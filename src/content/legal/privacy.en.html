What we receive

This site has no forms, accounts or newsletter. We don't ask for any information to browse it.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive what you send: usually your name, your number or email, and details about your stable and your horse. We use that only to reply and arrange your entry, and we don't sell it or share it for advertising. We keep those conversations as long as they're needed to organise the races.

WhatsApp is a Meta service, and Google Maps and Facebook are third-party services. If you use them from links on this site, their own privacy policies apply.

How the site works

The site is delivered through Amazon Web Services (AWS). To show you the pages, AWS processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type. We don't keep server access logs.

Fonts, images and videos are served from our own domain. No outside company receives your data just because you open a page.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

Only if you click "Accept" on the cookie notice do we load Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Advertising features and Google Signals are turned off, and data is deleted after 2 months. If you reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing loads.

We store your choice in a cookie named cd_consent for 6 months. You can change it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page; withdrawing deletes the Google Analytics cookies.
