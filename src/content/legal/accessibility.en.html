Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this site. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

Known limits

  • The video at the top of the home page is silent aerial footage. It has a pause button and doesn't play if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • On computers, the photo gallery scrolls on its own. You can pause it with its button, and it stops when the mouse or keyboard focus is on it. On phones and touch screens it doesn't move by itself: you swipe through the photos.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and Facebook lead to third-party services we don't control.
