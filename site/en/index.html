Hudson, Colorado · Horse racing

Three hundred yards. One winner.

Match races and open races on a six-lane track northeast of Denver. Bring your stable, or come see who gets there first.

Enter your horse See the races

How we race

Carreras Parejeras

Match races

Two horses, two stables, head to head. Distance and terms are agreed between the owners, and settled on the track.

Carreras Abiertas

Open races

Several horses from the same gate, open to every stable that enters for the day's card.

The track

A dirt track with a six-lane starting gate and white rails, with the Rocky Mountains in the distance.
